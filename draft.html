Patch notes

New

  • Filter the board by spec.
  • Your Runs keeps every run you finish, with its gear and damage breakdown.
  • Watch the draft: step through a run and see each item go on the character.
  • Share a link to one run or to all of your runs.

Fight changes

  • Warrior rage now follows Forever: it comes from swing speed and crits, not damage. Slam and Overpower also match Forever.
  • Life Tap now costs health, so Warlocks can run out of mana.
  • Mages now use Evocation and a Mana Ruby.
  • Hunter pets now take armor reduction.
  • Attack power that only works in Cat or Bear form now only counts for Feral Druids.
  • Item procs now use real proc rates. Thunderfury procs far more often, most items less.
  • Abilities can now trigger procs, not just white hits. Spells and shots trigger the items they should.
  • Armor reduction procs like Annihilator and Bonereaver's Edge now work.
  • Set bonuses now do what they say. The old flat bonus for sets is gone.
  • Stacking trinkets like Jom Gabbar and Zandalarian Hero Charm now stack and fade properly.
  • Hunters no longer get attack power from gear counted twice.
  • Summons now fight with you: Dragon's Call, Blackblade of Shahram, Book of the Dead, Ancient Cornerstone Grimoire and the Spirit of Eskhandar set. You can see them in the fight.
  • Survival Hunters now have a pet, and you can see Hunter pets in the fight. Items that boost pet damage now work. Marksmanship stays petless for Lone Wolf.
  • The fight has new spell, arrow and impact effects for each spec.
  • Base stats, race stats and mana regen now match each class.
  • A queued Heroic Strike or Raptor Strike no longer cancels the dual-wield miss penalty.

Items

  • Andonisus, Reaper of Souls is gone. It only lasts 5 minutes in the game, so it was never real loot.
  • Shard of the Defiler, Ashbringer and the two Talismans of Binding are gone. They were never obtainable in the game.

Old runs

  • Every run was re-scored with the new rules, so old and new runs compete fairly.
  • Runs that had a removed item got the next best item in that slot.
How the score works

Your character fights a dummy for two minutes, 12 times over, with no raid buffs or consumables. The dummy has 3,731 armor and the hit, dodge, glancing and crit rules of a boss three levels above you. Casters can run out of mana, just like in game. Life Tap costs health and nobody heals you, and Mages get one Evocation and one Mana Ruby. Warrior rage uses Forever's normalized rules: it comes from swing speed and crits, not damage. Gear stats, hit, crit, weapon skill, set bonuses, item procs and Use items all count. Procs use each item's own rate and can trigger from white hits, abilities, shots or spells, depending on the item. Use items fire on cooldown, so a 45 second cooldown goes off three times in a fight.

Builds come from Wowhead's Forever guides. Ability numbers are Classic Era, to match the Classic items, with Forever talents on top. Abilities new to Forever use estimated values, and rotations are simplified. Items come from Classic Era for now. Forever's own items join once their data is public.
