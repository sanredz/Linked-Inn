Linked Inn is a profession finder for WoW Forever. While you play it quietly reads the professions of crafters around you and shares lists between its users. These charts are what that adds up to: no surveys, no hand-picked numbers, just what crafters actually know.

Crafting professions only (Cooking, First Aid and Fishing are left out). "New to Forever" means recipes that don't exist in original Classic. "Blue" means the item it makes is rare quality. When comparing, numbers are shares of each group, so a realm type with fewer crafters still compares fairly; hollow markers mean a group is still a small sample. Linked Inn on GitHub.
