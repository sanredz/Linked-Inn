Privacy

Short version: this site keeps almost nothing about you, and only a handful of player names ever appear on it outside the Forever Open tab.

Visiting the site

There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no cookies and no ads. Fonts and images are served from this site.

To see how many people visit, the site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics. It counts anonymous page views (which page, the country, the kind of device and which site sent you here) without cookies, without tracking you across other sites and without storing anything personal.

Your browser remembers the realm type, faction and talent class you picked, so the page opens the way you left it. That stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, like any web host; see GitHub's own privacy statement for what they keep.

Voting on the Forever Open

When you vote for a tournament winner, the vote box keeps your pick together with a scrambled code made from your IP address, so each connection gets one vote. The IP address itself is never stored, and the code cannot be turned back into it. Once voting closes, those codes are wiped and only the totals are kept, so the site can show how the crowd's pick held up.

Where the numbers come from

Everything is read from World of Warcraft: Forever, as any player can see it in the game:

Crafting: professions and recipes of crafters, picked up by the Linked Inn addon and by players who send in their lists.

Duels: duel results that the game announces to everyone nearby, with each fighter's class, race, level and gear.

Life on Azeroth: the Statistics panel every player has, the same one anyone can open when comparing achievements.

Forever Open: the public tournament standings, matches and registered talents on Raider.IO.

Names

Names are left out everywhere, with three exceptions: the duelists at the very top (the champions and the best of each class), the Regulars who chose to have their name on the board when they sent in their list, and the Forever Open tab, which lists the tournament's entrants as they appear in its public standings.

If your name is on here and you would rather it was not, message sanredz on CurseForge or GitHub and it comes off, no questions asked.

Sending in your list

If you upload your Linked Inn file, it is used only to add crafters to the census. The name you give is shown on the Regulars board; leave it empty to stay anonymous.
